# Reference accounts: EATMEAT Games and Mix and Jam (reviewed 2026-10-18)

The dev likes both styles: a calm, earnest voice talking over carefully made features. I reviewed 12 Reels: downloaded each one,
transcribed it (whisper base.en), and made frame sheets at 1 fps plus the first 3 s at 4 fps.
Only view counts are public (no retention data), so treat this as a pattern across 12 videos, not proof.

## What the winners share
1. **A calm voice explains every winner. Every loser had no voice.** Across 12 videos the dividing line is narration. Music-only clips sat at 3-31K on both accounts. Narrated ones reached 66K-1.2M.
2. **One feature per video, explained by why it exists**, not just what it is: "I want *you* to get better", "where the real pressure lives". The why is what makes a feature feel crafted.
3. **The first sentence does the hook alone.** The patterns that worked:
   - a feeling the viewer has had ("You know that feeling when...");
   - a bold claim ("our most satisfying mechanic yet");
   - a big number ("6-7 thousand years");
   - a list that builds ("First we added speech, then...");
   - a gap ("Nobody made a game about X, so I did").
4. **Grounded in the real world**: "based on real conchoidal fracture physics", the real VAR room. It makes the detail credible.
5. **A thesis line to close**: "Mastery is the progression." "No skill trees, just player skill." It's quotable and positions the game against genre habits.
6. **The game's own sound is the payoff**: the whistle, the off-key flute, the stone clicks, the net ripple.
7. **Slow pace.**
   - Speech runs ~2-2.4 words/s, against 3+ for typical TikTok.
   - The 827K video is about 4 long drifting shots in 27 s.
   - Shots hold while the feature visibly does its thing.
8. **Longer than our rule said.** Winners ran 21-60 s, and Mix and Jam's best were 51-60 s. A calm narrated clip holds people longer than a 15 s montage.
9. **A closing question pulls comments** (688 on the 1.2M video). A question that's only bait, with nothing explained, flopped (31K).
10. **Re-cutting works.** The same whistling voice-over, mirrored with new captions and re-posted 2 weeks later, drew another 161K.
11. **Comparison pitches work.** "Papers, Please + Severance + football" got 124K, which supports "Gran Turismo 2 with a track editor".

## Caveats
- View counts only, from two accounts, and posting order is a confound: the whistling v2 was a re-post, so its lower number is partly expected.
- Caption style isn't decisive. Winners used both calm sentences and yellow word-highlight captions.
- I can't hear voice timbre from a transcript. The dev should listen to the whistling and VAR-room Reels for the tone itself.
