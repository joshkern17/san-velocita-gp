 Nobody recorded these engines. Every one of them is built out of pipes. Each cylinder is a short tube with a valve at each end. Every firing sends a pulse through the headers, the muffler, and out the tailpipe. The pipes keep their own notes. The revs just set how often they're hit. Change the pipes and you change the voice. Same eight cylinders, different headers. One barks, one burbles. The M1's inline six just keeps on climbing. And the Miata does its best. 15 engines. Every one starts as pipes and air. So bark or burble.
