# Explainer 001: tire temperatures

Format: narrated feature explainer (STRATEGY §4, main series). Pillars: DRV (sim driving) + CAR (the car).
Narrator: SV narrator 001 (`7HQTSYjhnOsl76FVM9QA`), eleven_v4. Target ~40 s plus a 3 s end card.

Fact check (docs/PHYSICS.md "Tyre temperatures"):
- Every tire has a surface (fast, from sliding) and a core (slow, from rolling).
- Grip blends them 65/35, inside each compound's window.
- More than 12 °C over the window, wear rises.
- F1 and GT1 cars fit tires from warmers; the rest start at air temperature + 5 °C. Road cars reach the window in about a lap.
