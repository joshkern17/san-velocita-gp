San Velocita
GRAND PRIX

Draw the track. Paint the car.
Race both through a career.

A PS1-era sim racer with real tire physics, real circuits scanned from LiDAR, and a career that starts in a spec Miata.

Coming to Steam. Sign up and we'll tell you when it's playable.

THE IDEA

Gran Turismo 2, with a track editor.

01

Make tracks

Draw a circuit in a minute and the AI learns it. Or race a real one, rebuilt from national LiDAR scans down to the camber in the corners.

02

Drive it yourself

Every tire has a surface and a core temperature. Grip peaks at a few degrees of slip, then slides away. The AI drives the same physics you do.

03

Your car

Club racers, GT2 and GT1 legends. Paint yours in the livery designer, sponsors and all, then take it racing.

04

Build a team

Start in a spec Miata series. Win, upgrade, move into a bigger garage, and climb through Street and GT2 to GT1.

05

Race together

Race your friends online. A shared co-op career, one team and one garage between you, is in the works.
