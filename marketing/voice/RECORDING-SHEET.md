# Recording sheet: "I made a game where…" (your voice)

**Setup:**
- Phone voice memo app, held about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly off to the side so your breath doesn't hit it.
- A room with soft things in it, like a closet full of clothes or a blanket over your head. A bare room echoes.
- Phone on airplane mode.

**How to read:**
- Talk like you're explaining it to a friend, not reading. Slower than feels natural, a bit under 2.5 words a second.
- Do **3 takes of each script**. Leave a second of silence before each, and say the script number at the start
  ("S1, take 2").
- If you trip, pause, then start that sentence again. I cut between takes, but only at whole sentences.
- Change any word that doesn't sound like you. They're your sentences.
- Drop the files in `marketing/voice/raw/`, any format.

## S1 · Draw a track, then race it
I always wanted to draw my own race track, and then drive it properly. Not a toy. Real tires, real braking points.
Every game I tried did one or the other. So I started making the one I wanted. You sketch the road, hit Race It, and
you're on the grid, against AI that practiced your corners before you got there. It's called San Velocita. I'm still
building it. What would you draw first?

## S2 · The racing game I miss
I miss racing games that looked like this. Low polygons, fog on the horizon. So I made one. But under the old look,
it's all new. Tires that warm up corner by corner. Engines built from air and pipes, not recordings. Real circuits,
scanned from laser data. The feeling I remember, with the depth I always wanted. It's called San Velocita. What's the
one you miss?

## S3 · Build a team with someone in your life
Most racing games put you up against the people in your life. I wanted one where we're on the same side. So in San
Velocita, you and a friend run one team. You walk the same garage. You share one pit box on race weekends, and take on
the same field together. I'm building the shared career now, so the team you grow is one you built together. Who would
you build a team with?

## Swap-in middles (one take each is fine)
- **S4:** Before every race, the AI runs practice laps in secret on whatever you drew, and learns how fast each corner
  can go. Rookie drives a share of that. Alien drives all of it.
- **S5:** Spa isn't modeled by eye here. Its hills and camber come from public laser scans of the ground, so Eau Rouge
  climbs the way it really does.
- **S6:** You don't start with a race car. You start with a Miata, a small garage and a friend who texts too much. Win,
  and the garage grows.

## Openings and close for the swaps (S4-S6)
- "I made a game where the AI practices your track before you race it."
- "I made a game where the tracks are scanned from real laser data."
- "I made a game where your race team starts with one tired Miata."
- Close: "It's called San Velocita. I'm still building it."
