## 2. About this game

Steam recommends images and GIFs **1170 px wide**. Animated GIF, WEBM or MP4 assets can run **12 s at most**, and each

**[GIF 1 · MAKE TRACKS · 10 s: the editor, a road sketched with the mouse → RACE IT → the field going into the first
corner of that track. Needs director editor capture: see TRAILER.md "What the director needs".]**

### Draw it. Race it.
Sketch a road with the mouse, or place it point by point. Shape the hills and the banking, then lay curbs, barriers,
gravel, runoff and a pit lane. Press Race It and you're on the grid.

Before the green flag, the AI drives practice laps nobody sees, so it already knows your corners when you get there.
Trace your circuit over a real map of your own town, with its streets, buildings and hills pulled in. Save it, send the
file to a friend, or host it in an online race.

### Or race the real ones
Fourteen real circuits, rebuilt from open map data. Most take their elevation and camber from national LiDAR and
laser-survey data, so the Corkscrew drops the way it does in real life and the climb out of Eau Rouge feels like a
wall.

**ALT, without circuit names:** "…so the famous downhill corkscrew drops the way it does in real life and the climb
through the Belgian forest feels like a wall."

---

**[GIF 2 · YOU DRIVE IT · 10 s: chase cam with the HUD on, the tire temperature readout going from blue to green over
a lap's first corners, then a car tucking into another's tow on a straight.]**

### You drive it
Your tires have a temperature on the surface and another in the core, and they grip best inside a window. Push too
hard and they overheat. Follow too close and you lose front downforce in the dirty air, then get it back as a tow on
the straight.

- **Tires:** soft, medium, hard, wet and dirt compounds, with wear front and rear.
- **Damage:** bent suspension, an overheating engine and lost gears on Realistic.
- **Setup:** a full setup with saved versions for every car: wings, springs, dampers, camber, toe, diff and every
  gear ratio.
- **Controls:** a gamepad, a wheel or a keyboard, with assists that run from Easy to Sim.
- **Conditions:** day, dusk and night, and clear skies, rain and fog, with rain that can arrive mid-race.

### Rivals who race you
The AI plans its passes. It sits in your tow, pulls out, dives down the inside, and moves over for a blue flag
when you come up to lap it. Six difficulty levels, a safety car, track limits and penalties.

---

**[GIF 3 · YOUR CAR · 8 s: the livery designer, a pattern change, a decal placed by clicking the car, then that car on
track in the race.]**

### Your car, your colors
Pick a pattern and your colors, then place decals by clicking on the car. Use the in-game sponsors or your own PNG and
SVG files, or draw shapes straight onto the bodywork. Design the helmet and the race suit too. Online, everyone sees
what you made.

---

**[GIF 4 · CAREER · 10 s: walking the home garage (the lock-up), the week planner, then a club race start.]**

### Start at the bottom
World Career puts you in charge of a team at the bottom of club racing, with a rented lock-up, a car and a phone that
won't stop buzzing. Hire the staff, find sponsors, pick a teammate and choose how to spend the week before each race.
Climb through the Street Cup, GT2 and GT1.

The garage grows with you, from the lock-up to a workshop and then a factory, and you walk around it while your crew
works on the car. Your engineer talks you through the race on the radio. GT seasons include a six-hour race, sharing
the car with your teammate in a mixed-class field.

---

**[GIF 5 · TOGETHER · 8 s: two players' cars side by side online, then two drivers walking the same garage.]**

### Bring a friend
Race online with up to eight drivers. Share a room code, bring your own tracks and liveries, and fill the rest of the
grid with AI. Walk the same garage together between races. A shared co-op career, one team run by two people, is in
the works.

## 4. Genre and categories
- **Genre:** Racing, Simulation, Indie. Steam's genre checkboxes are separate from tags, so Indie is fine here.
- **Players:**
  - Single-player: yes.
  - Multi-player, PvP and Online PvP: yes, online races for up to 8 drivers.
  - Online Co-op: **not yet**. Tick it when the shared career ships. Racing in the same online race is PvP.
  - Shared/Split Screen, LAN: no.
- **Controller:** **Partial Controller Support.** Racing, the menus and the garage work on a pad, but the track editor
  and the livery designer need a mouse.
- **Wheels:** the game recognizes Thrustmaster, Logitech, Fanatec, Moza, Simagic and Cammus wheels and has a setup
  wizard. **Don't claim force feedback**, because it needs a separate helper program today. The controller notes field:
  "Steering wheels supported (setup wizard included)."
- **Steam features:**
  - Steam Cloud, Workshop and Achievements: no, none are built.
  - Remote Play Together: no.
- **Languages:** English (interface, full audio and subtitles). The engineer's radio is English audio.

## Claims checked (2026-10-19, against the working tree)
- **Draw freehand or by points, then Race It with AI:** yes (editor.js, Race It button). The AI learns any track with
  hidden laps (`learnLimits`).
- **Hills, banking, curbs, barriers, gravel/runoff, pit lane:** yes (editor tools).
- **Real map of your town, its streets, buildings and hills:** yes (geo.js, terrain.js). It needs an internet connection.
