Braking

Brake in a straight line before the corner, then roll the throttle on as you unwind the steering. Turn the racing line on (L) to see where to brake: it turns red.

Slipstream

Tuck in behind another car on a straight for extra speed. SLIPSTREAM lights up above your speed when it's working.

Tyres

Soft is fastest but wears quickest, Medium and Hard last longer, Wet is for rain. Below 30% the tyre falls off a cliff.

Pit stops

Drive into the pit entry and the crew drives you to the box. Pick tyres and repairs with the arrows or d-pad on the way in.

Damage

Contact breaks wings (less grip) and the chassis (less speed). The car diagram next to your speed shows what's hurt. Repairs are done in the pits.

Drifting

Road and GT cars drift: flick the handbrake into a corner, then hold the angle with the throttle: more throttle for more angle, ease off to tighten, and counter-steer to trim it. Turn on Drift Points in Quick Race to score it.

Race weekends

Set Weekend in the race settings (Career and Grand Prix have it too): qualifying, or practice then qualifying, before the race. Sessions start in your garage: go out on a flying lap or down the pit lane, skip ahead, drive into the pits to come back in. Damage and worn tyres stay on the car: repairs and new tyres cost session time.

Delta

In qualifying and time trial the DELTA under your lap times compares you with your best lap as you drive: green, you're up on it; red, you're down.

Presets

Easy brakes and catches slides for you, Standard keeps ABS, traction and stability control, Sim turns everything off. Set each aid in Options › Aids, even mid-race from the pause menu.

The aid lights

The chips under the rev bar show the aids you have on. One flashes gold when it steps in: that's the car saving you, and a hint about what to do better.

ABS & braking

ABS stops the wheels locking. Without it, squeeze the brake: stamp on it and the fronts lock, skid, and won't steer until you ease off. Braking Assist brakes for you if you arrive at a corner too fast.

Traction & stability

TC cuts power when the rears spin up on the way out of a corner. SC stops the car rotating further than the tyres can hold. With both off, be gentle with the throttle.

Steering help

Counter-Steer Help points the wheels into a slide for you, and backs off when you counter-steer yourself. Steering Assist stops you turning in so hard the front tyres give up.

Prize bonus

In Career, every aid you turn off pays extra prize money, up to about +35% with Sim. The lowest setting you raced with counts, so turning aids back on mid-race lowers it.

Controller feel

With Vibration on you feel the fronts scrubbing, the rear sliding, wheelspin, ABS, lock-ups and kerbs. Set the stick's response, deadzone and speed in Options › Controller.

Online

The host can limit aids for everyone: no auto-brake or steering assist, or Sim only. It shows on the lobby's circuit card.

Yellow & green

Yellow: a car has spun, stopped or gone off. No overtaking in that sector, and slow right down for a double yellow. Green: all clear. Watch the flag board, the marshal panels and the minimap.

Give the place back

Passed someone under yellow or the VSC? Let them back past before the timer on the flag board runs out, or it's a 5 second penalty.

Blue flag

You're about to be lapped. Let the faster car through soon. Hold them up for too long and it's a penalty. AI cars move over for you the same way.

Virtual Safety Car

After a big crash or a car stuck on the track. Everyone slows down. Keep the delta on the flag board positive (green) and don't overtake. Pitting under the VSC costs less time.

Black & orange

A wing has come off. Pit for repairs within 2 laps. Ignore it and it's a drive-through (Realistic rules: the black flag).

Track limits

Cutting the inside of a corner deletes that lap, and in a race it's a warning. Running wide is forgiven on Arcade unless you go well off and keep your speed. Realistic: the white line is the limit. Too many warnings: black and white flag, then penalties.

Collisions

Run into the back of a car and the stewards take a look. If they spin, go off or get damaged, it's a 5 second penalty. Side-by-side contact is a racing incident.

Penalties

Time: served at your next stop or added at the flag. Drive-through: drive through the pits within 3 laps. Set the strictness with Race Rules, and how often the AI slips up with AI Mistakes.

Host a game

Pick Host Game to open a room. Send the 5-letter room code to your friends. You choose the circuit, laps, AI and weather in the lobby.

Join a game

Type the room code on the Online screen and press Enter. Mark yourself Ready in the lobby.

In the race

Online races can't be paused: the menu opens while your car slows down. Session points are kept for every race you do together.

Same version

Everyone needs the same copy of the game. If your friend is on Windows, send them the single-file build.
